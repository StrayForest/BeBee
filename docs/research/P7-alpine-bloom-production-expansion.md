# P7 Alpine Bloom — production expansion research

## Scope

Alpine Bloom is the fifth authored region in the canonical sequence. This slice tests whether the accepted continuous-world restoration pattern can carry a colder, highland identity through Snowdrop Pass, Anemone Ridge, Wind Carve and Alpine Crown while retaining movement-through pollination, Honey, Flight/Buzz 3, save v4, analytics and the six-slot nearby-patch presentation.

The slice explicitly excludes a new currency, core verb, mandatory spend, lethal terrain, per-region proxy lifecycle, procedural generation and release-candidate art certification.

## Candidate pool

- **A Short Hike** — https://ashorthike.com/ — landmark-led traversal keeps a compact authored space legible.
- **Alba: A Wildlife Adventure** — https://www.albawildlife.com/ — habitat identity comes from local composition and low-pressure objectives.
- **Garden Life: A Cozy Simulator** — https://store.steampowered.com/app/1915380/Garden_Life_A_Cozy_Simulator/ — authored restoration and player expression provide a useful content-scaling comparison.
- **Spiritfarer** — https://thunderlotusgames.com/spiritfarer/ — counter-model for adding management/crafting surfaces beyond the BeBee loop.
- **Yonder: The Cloud Catcher Chronicles** — https://www.yonderchronicles.com/ — counter-model for broad biome systems whose breadth is outside this slice.

## Selected deep references

### A Short Hike — authored route and landmarks

- Source: https://ashorthike.com/
- Checked: 2026-08-30.
- Observation: a compact handcrafted world is navigated through recognizable local places and traversal rhythm.
- Inference: Alpine Bloom should make Snowdrop Pass, Anemone Ridge, Wind Carve and Alpine Crown the route language without adding a map or management screen.
- BeBee measurements: four new authored Meadows; zero new navigation screens.

### Alba: A Wildlife Adventure — habitat distinction

- Source: https://www.albawildlife.com/
- Checked: 2026-08-30.
- Observation: authored habitats communicate identity through environmental context, silhouettes and local objectives.
- Inference: Edelweiss/Anemone families, rock/snow/wind accents and a cool palette can distinguish Alpine Bloom while pollination remains the only restoration verb.
- BeBee measurements: zero new core verbs, zero new currencies and zero lethal hazards.

## Counter-model

Spiritfarer is intentionally treated as an anti-pattern for this slice: its management, crafting, building and relationship surfaces would turn a regional content expansion into a new product layer. Alpine Bloom instead reuses the existing patch, Honey, upgrade and save contracts.

## Technical constraints

Official Defold references checked 2026-08-30:

- https://defold.com/manuals/collection-proxy/ — no per-region proxy lifecycle is needed for this authored extension.
- https://defold.com/manuals/collection-factory/ — no new collection lifecycle is introduced before measured evidence requires it.
- https://defold.com/manuals/gui/ — retain max_nodes=512 and a reusable six-slot nearby-patch visual pool.
- https://defold.com/manuals/optimization-memory/ — keep the catalog and serialized view state bounded and measure the release/browser result.

## BeBee decision

Select one continuous authored Alpine Bloom region with four Meadows, two alpine flower families and finite expanded movement/camera bounds. Keep save v4 and platform-neutral analytics unchanged. Carry the existing geometric illustration/typography/animation/full-scene deviation to P8 visual certification rather than treating it as a functional ITERATE.
