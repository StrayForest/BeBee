BeBee privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-08-30.

BeBee does not operate a player account or a BeBee analytics server. Local progression is stored by the game runtime in the browser's local storage layer.

Optional product telemetry is disabled by default and is sent only after explicit consent and provider configuration. It contains stable event names and aggregate gameplay fields, never names, email addresses, precise location, raw text input, authentication tokens or advertising identifiers.

The selected portal may process technical data and advertising events under its own privacy policy. Platform gameplay lifecycle calls are used to keep portal play-state reporting correct and do not carry player identity.

Players can continue playing without optional telemetry consent. Resetting browser site data removes local progression.

Privacy questions can be raised through the project repository: github.com/StrayForest/BeBee/issues.
